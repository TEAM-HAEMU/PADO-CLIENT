Thank you for reviewing PADO (파도).

LOGIN
- The fastest way in is "Sign in with Apple" on the first screen (Kakao, Google and email sign-up are also available). After first login you will see a short sign-up form (nickname, date of birth, and optional gender / preferred listening app) and the terms consent screen.

WHAT THE APP DOES
- Users leave ("drop") a song at their current location; nearby users discover these drops as pins on the map and can play a 30-second preview.
- Previews are provided by the Apple iTunes Search API (attribution shown as "미리듣기 제공 · Apple Music"). "전체 듣기" (full listen) opens the song in the user's chosen app (Spotify / Apple Music / YouTube Music) via deep link. We do not host or stream full tracks.

LOCATION
- The pre-permission screen now has a single "계속" (Continue) button that always proceeds to the system location permission dialog; there is no skip option.
- Location (When In Use) is required to show nearby drops and to place a drop. Drops are shown within a limited radius, so the map may look empty near Cupertino. To test, tap the drop (+) button in the bottom dock, search any song and drop it at your location; it will then appear on the map.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
- Report: on any drop, tap ⋯ → "신고하기" (report drop) or "사용자 신고" (report user). On comments, long-press a comment → "신고하기" / "차단하기".
- Block: ⋯ → "차단하기". Blocked users' drops and comments disappear immediately. Manage at Profile → Settings → 차단한 사용자.
- Reports are reviewed by the operator within 24 hours; violating content is removed and the user may be suspended. Users must agree to the terms (no objectionable content) before using the app.

ACCOUNT DELETION
- Profile → Settings → 회원 탈퇴. Apple sign-in tokens are revoked on deletion.

ANALYTICS
- The app does not track users and shows no ads. The custom analytics consent prompt has been removed; usage analytics (Amplitude, first-party only) is off by default and can only be turned on by the user in Profile → Settings → 이용 데이터 분석.
